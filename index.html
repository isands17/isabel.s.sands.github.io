About

I am a School of Science Dean's Postdoctoral Fellow at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where I am a member of the MIT Kavli Institute for Astrophysics and Space Research. My work sits at the intersection of theoretical physics, cosmology, and our understanding of the large-scale universe.

My research focuses on the theoretical underpinnings of astro-particle physics and the physical processes that govern galaxy formation and evolution across cosmic time. I am interested in how the properties of dark matter and dark energy leave observable imprints on the distribution of matter in the universe.
