Overview

I work at the intersection of theoretical particle physics and astrophysics, developing models and simulations that connect fundamental physics to observable signatures across cosmic scales. My projects span cosmic ray phenomenology in galaxy simulations, the indirect detection of dark matter through gravitational and radiative probes, and the cosmological signatures of physics beyond the standard ΛCDM model.

I frequently take on new projects as compelling questions arise, so this page reflects my current and recent work rather than a fixed research program.
